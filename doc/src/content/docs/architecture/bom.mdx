This is the **final** BOM, not a candidate list. Every C-number below was re-verified
individually against the local JLCPCB parts database (snapshot dated **2026-07-25**;
per-part data-fetch dates within the snapshot range from 2026-05-23 to 2026-07-03).
The XFCN `JOUT1`/`J2` mating pair was separately checked against live JLCPCB/LCSC
inventory and manufacturer drawings on **2026-08-20**; its rows carry that date.

This page intentionally covers the two assembled lamp boards. The separate
[ST-LINK/V2 SWD adapter](./swd-adapter.mdx) has only two hand-fit/DNP connector
lines, `C4749199` and `C492404`; their current identity and evidence live in the
component catalog rather than the lamp-PCBA cost arithmetic below.

Every fitted line below also has a canonical component record — the exact orderable
identity, every board placement, the sources behind it and which coverage domains are
still open. Browse them in the [component catalog](/docs/components/catalog/); the
cross-component rules that span several of these parts at once are on the
[integration page](/docs/components/integration/). This page keeps the ordering,
tier and cost reasoning, which lives nowhere else.

:::warning[Stock is a point-in-time read]
Stock figures are from the snapshot, not a guarantee. Re-check every line immediately
before ordering — and pay particular attention to the four lines flagged below with
stock under 25,000. Tier assignments (Basic / Preferred / Extended) can also change; the
fee arithmetic at the bottom of this page is only as good as the tier data on the day
you order.
:::

## Sourcing risks

Four lines carry stock low enough to matter on a small order. None is a design flaw —
they are the specialty parts — but each needs a re-check before ordering.

| Part | Stock | Risk and fallback |
|---|---|---|
| D5 / D10 SMAJ20A ([C571370](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/C571370)) | **3,770**, and **two boards need it** | Lowest-stock line in the whole design. Do not make a same-name or different-standoff substitution: exact vendor identity, conditioned clamp table, pad orientation, and both rail waveforms must be re-audited in the [ratings analysis](/docs/power/ratings-matrix/) and against the canonical record ([`rec-high-diode-smaj20a`](/docs/components/records/high-diode-smaj20a/)) |
| U2 AL8860MP-13 ([C500782](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/C500782)) — canonical record [`rec-al8860mp-13`](/docs/components/records/al8860mp-13/) | **4,000** | Fine for a prototype run. `AL8860WT-7` (TSOT-25) is pin-compatible in function but showed 0 stock and is limited to 1 A — it would still cover this design's 500 mA if the MSOP part disappears |
| RV1 RK10J11E0034 / C470643 | **6,939** | Current stopped-pot choice; do not substitute an encoder without circuit/firmware revision |
| RS1 RLP25FEER200 ([C459674](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/C459674)) | **20,654** | Any 200 mΩ ±1% 2512 sense resistor rated 1 W or better substitutes directly; the value, not the manufacturer, is what sets 500 mA — see [`fact-rlp25-resistance`](/docs/components/records/rlp25feer200/#fact-rlp25-resistance) |

## Extended-part fee economics

JLCPCB charges a one-time setup fee — **~¥470 (roughly $3)** — per unique **Extended**
part number, per order. Basic and Preferred parts do not incur it. The fee is per order,
so a part used on both boards is charged **twice** when the boards are ordered as two
separate PCBA jobs, which they must be.

| | Board P | Board L |
|---|---|---|
| Unique fitted part numbers | 14 | 22 |
| of which **Basic** | 9 | 8 |
| of which **Preferred** | 0 | 1 |
| of which **Extended** | **5** | **13** |

Board P Extended lines: `C2678061`, `C347476`, `C283540`, `C571370`, `C492405` — one
fewer than the original plan, because the C1711 → C14663 100 nF swap was applied.

Board L Extended lines: `C500782`, `C529334`, `C780769`, `C571370`, `C177245`,
`C167874`, `C459674`, `C2934070`, `C13564`, `C976305`, `C2832269`, `C2991196`, `C210315`.

| | Value |
|---|---|
| Extended lines charged | 5 + 13 = **18** |
| Setup fee total | 18 x ¥470 ≈ **¥8,460** (roughly **$54**) |
| Component cost, both boards, single-unit quantity | roughly **$8** |

**The setup fees are about seven times the parts cost.** That is the shape of every
small JLCPCB PCBA order and it is worth internalising before optimising anything else:
one avoided Extended line saves more than the entire resistor and capacitor content of
both boards combined. It also amortises to nothing on a reorder — a second batch of the
same design pays no setup fee at all.

Two reductions were already taken:

- **Board L's 10 µF caps use [C13585](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/C13585) (1206, Basic)
  rather than the 1210 X7R part the driver research listed
  ([C2918502](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/C2918502), Extended).** Saves ¥470 and shares
  a reel with Board P. The trade is X5R instead of the X7R the AL8860 datasheet asks for
  — X5R is rated to +85 °C, which covers this lamp's envelope, but if a build shows input
  ripple problems, C2918502 is the datasheet-strict swap.
- **Both boards' 100 nF caps now share [C14663](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/C14663)
  (0603, Basic).** Board P originally specified [C1711](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/C1711)
  (0805, Extended); the swap was applied at KiCad capture and is already counted in the
  arithmetic above.

Two more are available if the budget matters:

- Dropping `RT1` (NTC) and `F1` (PPTC) would save ¥940 and remove the thermal-derate
  and over-current protections. Both were added deliberately; this is the price of them.
- Ordering both boards as a single panelised PCBA job, if JLCPCB permits it for the
  design, would collapse the shared `C571370` TVS line from two charges to one — ¥470
  saved. The two connector halves are different orderables and do not collapse.

## Parts explicitly not on this BOM

| Not present | Why |
|---|---|
| Aluminum MCPCB / heatsink | 0.19 W per LED site is well inside the plain-FR-4 regime — see [board split](./overview.mdx#board-split) |
| Status LED | Would add current to the logic rail for information the lamp itself already conveys |
| Crystal oscillator | HSI16 + PLL at 64 MHz is far more accurate than anything this design's timing needs |
| BOOT0 strapping resistor | The factory-default option bit `nBOOT_SEL = 1` makes the G0 boot from flash and ignore the BOOT0 pin entirely (the pin is only consulted if `nBOOT_SEL` is cleared) — so no strap is needed, but flipping `nBOOT_SEL` in firmware would change that |
| External Rd on CC lines | Deliberately DNP — the single most consequential rule from [zudo-pd's diagnosis](/docs/power/lessons-from-zudo-pd/#cc-topology-rules) |
| SWD/UART connectors | Footprint only; hand-fit for bring-up |
