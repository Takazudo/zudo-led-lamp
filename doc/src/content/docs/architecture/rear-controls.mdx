The 24 LEDs face the light chamber. On the opposite face, **RV1 ALPS
RK10J11E0034 / C470643** provides absolute brightness with physical stops, and
**SW2 G-Switch SS-12D01-G020 / C5446803** provides maintained power ON/OFF.
The tall encoder and toggle are superseded.

![Actual Board L rear-control render](/assets/pcb/board-l-rear-controls.png)

## Placement and height

Coordinates use KiCad's front-view axes on the 60 × 60 mm Board L outline.
An underside view mirrors X; do not mirror these coordinates again in mechanical CAD.

| Feature | X (mm) | Y (mm) | Placement |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| RV1 footprint origin | 51.90 | 9.50 | B.Cu, 0° |
| RV1 wheel centre | 51.90 | 12.00 | Offset from footprint origin |
| SW2 footprint origin | 9.10 | 27.75 | B.Cu, 90° |
| J2 centre | 29.40 | 37.70 | Existing six-contact mating alignment |

RV1's knob is **Ø14 ±0.2 mm**, with approximately **1.97 mm model reach** above
its mounting plane. Use the entire wheel envelope, not just the footprint origin,
for enclosure access and Board P clearance. The manufacturer lists insertion
`(t:2 mm)`; the project PCB is 1.6 mm. Catalog wording alone does not qualify
retention, seating or soldering on this thickness. Check the actual part and
opposite-face lead protrusion before fabrication release; do not silently change
all board thicknesses to match an ambiguous catalog label.

The imported ALPS model has a dual-unit family name but its external geometry is
checked against the selected single-unit drawing. The single-unit electrical
mapping is separate: five lower positions include endpoints 1/3, central wiper 2,
and two dummy terminals. Two additional upper supports complete seven physical
mounting features. All four dummy/support terminals remain electrically isolated.

SW2's corrected G020 family model uses a 3.5 mm case, 2 mm actuator and retained
0.5 mm shoulder: approximately **6 mm mounted height**. Reserve actuator travel,
finger access and clearance from the table. Family CAD is an enclosure aid, not
proof of exact manufactured dimensions or assembly fit.

## Board P and the pillars remain fixed

The Board P outline occupies **X 16.50–43.50, Y 0–40 mm** in Board L coordinates.
The shared upper pillars fix the transform `xL = xP + 16.50`, `yL = yP`.
Board P's holes (4,4) and (23,4) align with Board L's (20.5,4) and (39.5,4).
The third Board P hole projects to (29.3,32.6) and is not a shared through-hole.
Board P, pillars and J2 orientation/alignment are unchanged by this control revision.

![Rear projection of the two-board stack and controls](/assets/pcb/board-l-stack-clearance.svg)

J2 pin 1 remains at X23.05 and pin 6 at X35.75, all at Y37.70 mm. Confirm all six
mating contacts, pillar height and connector seating on the assembly. Check both
rear body clearance and front lead/solder clearance against LEDs and the chamber.

## Electrical and firmware handoff

RV1.1 is GND, RV1.3 is V3P3 and RV1.2 is BRIGHT_WIPER. R27 (100 Ω) feeds
BRIGHT_ADC at PA0/ADC_IN0; R22 (100 kΩ) and C22 (100 nF) shunt that node to GND.
R23/R28/C23 are removed and PA1 is unconnected. See [Board L](./board-l.mdx#knob-mapping)
for calibration, averaging, direction and startup requirements.

SW2 common 2 receives V15_FUSED, throw 1 feeds V15, and throw 3 is isolated on
the PCB but **live in OFF**. Board P remains powered. The
[power-switch procedure](./power-switch.mdx) retains the back-power, switching
transient and rail-decay checks.

Run KiCad DRC with zone refill and schematic parity, plus the control-placement
checker, after the final routed board is saved. Record that revision's actual
results; this handoff does not assert new DRC counts. Prototype gates remain
mechanical fit, absolute ADC endpoint/direction checks, acquisition settling,
smooth dimming, safe startup, thermal behavior and assembled OFF operation.
