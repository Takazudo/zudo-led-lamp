Board L now supports the intended interaction: turn the lamp over to reach the
power rocker and brightness knob. The 24 LEDs remain on the front, facing upward
into the starburst light chamber. SW1, the existing **EC11L1525G01 encoder**, is
mounted on the back, beside the pillar-mounted power board. It is not an analog potentiometer; its A/B/C connections and
unused D/E push contacts retain the schematic assignments.

## The rocker connects through J5

The board now routes `F1.2 → J5.1` on `V15_FUSED`, and `J5.2 → V15` to the existing
converter input distribution. The previous copper connection from F1 directly to
V15 is removed. Fit the external rocker between J5 pads 1 and 2 using the
[locked wiring and harness specification](./power-switch.mdx#where-the-switch-connects).
The square pad is **1 FEED**, and the round pad is **2 RETURN**; rear silkscreen
labels identify them. Both wires carry positive supply, not ground.

The switch feed and return remain routed through J5. The front LEDs retain their
positions. R31 moves from (43.25, 13.25) to (40.50, 13.25) mm to clear the rear
encoder's through-hole support lug; its two LED_P branches and LED_S2_A path are
rerouted. SW1's A/B/C connections retain their schematic identities.

Board L J2 is rotated 180 degrees from its former placement, then rerouted,
so numbered contacts align with Board P in the pillar-defined assembly. Its
centre remains (29.40, 37.70) mm. Pin 1 now lies at X 23.05 mm, and pin 6 at
X 35.75 mm, all at Y 37.70 mm. This corrects the previous reversed physical
pin order; the schematic signal assignments do not change. J2.5 uses a solid
connection to the rear ground plane instead of a starved thermal relief.

## Use these coordinates for the enclosure prototype

All coordinates below are **millimetres in KiCad's front-view coordinate system**,
measured from the upper-left corner of the 60 × 60 mm Board L outline. Do not
mirror these coordinates again when importing the board into mechanical CAD.
A drawing viewed from underneath reverses left and right.

| Feature | X | Y | Side / purpose |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| SW1 shaft / footprint origin | 51.50 | 12.50 | Back; encoder body and shaft extend below Board L |
| J5 pad 1 | 8.50 | 28.00 | Back wire entry; fused feed to WR11AS lug 1a |
| J5 pad 2 | 13.58 | 28.00 | Back wire entry; switched return from lug 1b |
| SW2 panel opening | Not fixed | Not fixed | External bottom panel; 34.2 × 17.2 mm, +0.3 / −0 mm |

The starburst-panel references establish hidden controls below a horizontal LED
board, but contain no dimensioned underside or enclosure CAD. The rocker opening
and the base depth therefore remain mechanical prototype decisions. Its specified
panel thickness is 1–4 mm; the encoder's panel and shaft fit must be checked against
the actual purchased part because the retained encoder mechanical evidence remains
[mirror-only and unresolved](/docs/components/records/c2991196/).

Provide a recessed bottom control area with finger access. The base rim or feet
must support the lamp with clearance below the installed knob and the rocker in
both positions. Include clearance for solder lugs, insulated wires, strain relief,
encoder hardware and removal of the bottom cover. A PCB render alone does not
establish those clearances.

### The pillars and connector fix Board P's position

The two upper Board P pillar holes, (4, 4) and (23, 4) mm, align with Board L's
(20.5, 4) and (39.5, 4) mm holes. This fixes the in-plane assembly transform:
**`xL = xP + 16.50`, `yL = yP`**, with no in-plane rotation. Board P's
27 × 40 mm outline therefore occupies **X 16.50–43.50, Y 0–40 mm** inside
Board L's outline. The existing pillar positions and Board P PCB are preserved.
Board P's third hole projects to (29.30, 32.60) mm; it is not a third shared
through-hole in Board L.

![Rear assembly projection showing Board P between the controls, the shared pillars and the aligned six-pin connector](/assets/pcb/board-l-stack-clearance.svg)

This diagram is viewed from underneath, so X is mirrored relative to the coordinate
table. The Board P outline is also marked on Board L's `Dwgs.User` layer.
SW1's footprint clears the power-board outline by approximately **1.39 mm**;
J5's pads clear it by **1.42 mm**. The verification script reserves a 1 mm
horizontal placement margin. This is a PCB envelope check, not clearance for a
chosen knob diameter or enclosure wall.

With the corrected J2 orientation, all six numbered contact centres match
JOUT1 after the same pillar-derived translation. Confirm seating, pillar height,
connector body clearance and six-net continuity in the physical assembly before
powering it. Place the external rocker and harness outside the power board's
occupied volume; its final panel coordinates and the enclosure depth remain
mechanical prototype decisions.

## Verification covers copper, not the assembled lamp

KiCad 10 loads, saves and renders the edited board. DRC is run with the existing
project rules, zone refill and schematic parity checking. The control checker
also verifies schematic footprint identities and pad-net assignments, rear SW1/J5,
front LEDs, all six pillar-aligned mating contacts, clearance around Board P, and J5's exclusion from orderable BOM and placement files.

```sh
kicad-cli pcb drc --format json --schematic-parity --refill-zones \
  -o /tmp/board-l-controls-drc.json boards/board-l/board-l.kicad_pcb
python3 scripts/pcb/verify-controls.py \
  --drc-report /tmp/board-l-controls-drc.json
```

The original board had 58 silkscreen warnings, no unrouted connections and two
schematic parity issues from the missing switch interface. The updated board has
no electrical errors, no unrouted connections and no schematic parity issues.
It retains the exact same 58 pre-existing silkscreen warning signatures, with no
new warnings. Review those existing warnings before a fabrication release.

Prototype checks remain: mechanical fit with both boards and the purchased controls,
clockwise brightness increase when viewed from the user-facing underside, stable
contact/debounce behaviour, OFF rail decay and back-power, switching inrush and
thermal behaviour inside the enclosure. Retain the existing
[power-switch bring-up procedure](./power-switch.mdx); DRC does not close its
`NEEDS BENCH` gates.
