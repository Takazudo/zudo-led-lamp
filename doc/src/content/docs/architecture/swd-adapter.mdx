# ST-LINK/V2 SWD adapter

`boards/swd-adapter/` is a separate 36 × 18.1 mm, two-layer PCB. It contains
exactly two hand-fit/DNP connectors and no active or passive circuitry:

- J1: DEALON `DS254P-2X10-L0` / `C4749199`, 2×10 shrouded 2.54 mm IDC header.
- J2: XFCN `PZ254V-11-05P` / `C492404`, unkeyed 1×5 2.54 mm header.

The exact connector evidence, drawing, footprint preview, model, and unresolved
bench checks are published in [`rec-c4749199`](/docs/components/records/c4749199/).
Board L's 1×5 order remains published in
[`rec-c492404`](/docs/components/records/c492404/).

## Locked mapping

| ST-LINK/V2 J1 | Adapter net | Board-side J2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 | SWDIO | 1 |
| 9 | SWCLK | 2 |
| 15 | NRST | 3 |
| 1, 2 | VAPP target-voltage sense | 4 |
| 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20 | GND | 5 |
| 11, 13 (SWO), 17, 19 | no connect | — |

This mapping targets the genuine ST-LINK/V2 CN3 interface documented in
[UM1075 Rev 10](https://www.st.com/resource/en/user_manual/um1075-stlink-v2-in-circuit-debugger-stlink-v2-1-stlink-v2-ct-stlink-v3-pg00061083.pdf).
The repository could not retain exact bytes from that URL, so the frozen
component evidence conservatively keeps the interface interpretation
`UNSOURCED` even though the generated schematic and PCB lock the mapping.

## Connection and power warning

Use a straight, pin-for-pin 1×5 female-to-female cable from adapter J2 to Board
L J3. Both 1×5 headers are unkeyed: align the square copper pad and `PIN 1`
silkscreen marks at both ends. Align the 20-pin ribbon's keyed plug with J1's
central shroud notch and pin-1 triangle.

The target must supply its own 3.3 V rail. J1 pins 1 and 2 only let ST-LINK
sense that target voltage. J1 pin 19 is the debugger's 3.3 V output and is
intentionally isolated; the adapter must never use it to power Board L.

## Verification state

The schematic regenerates deterministically, its exported netlist matches all
25 connector pins, and KiCad DRC reports no violations, unconnected items, or
schematic-parity issues. Fabrication-dependent acceptance remains `NEEDS BENCH`:
verify physical mating, every signal and ground continuity path, pin 19
isolation, absence of a VAPP-to-GND short, target-voltage sensing, and a real
debug/programming session.
