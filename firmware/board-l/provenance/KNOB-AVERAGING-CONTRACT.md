## Latency relation

For N explicit samples with stable qualified period T, fully populated history and sustained step from old raw A to new raw B: after k fresh new samples (1<=k<=N), mean=floor(((N-k)A+kB)/N). First output change may occur on first accepted new sample, but quantization/clamping may defer a very small change; no universal nonzero response bound for subcode motion. Full raw mean reaches B by Nth accepted sample: up to N*T from arbitrarily phased physical step under ideal periodic capture (plus analog/ADC/provider/control/output delays). Group delay for an ideal linear boxcar is (N-1)*T/2; this is not a guaranteed step-settling or perceptual limit. Warmup adapts to current real count; age eviction can shorten history, not prolong it past max_age.

Existing core ramp may add delay. For upward normalized delta D and explicit increment I, conservative serial upper allocation is (N+ceil(D/I)-1)*T plus service/analog terms, though actual ramp can progress while mean rises. Downward/off knob steps also average; thermal denial does NOT. A larger N therefore changes ordinary knob-to-zero response, but zero is not reinitialization. No numeric N or T is selected for production. HOST N4 and1..32 sweep are test configurations only. V71 conditional40/100ms or60/120ms proposals are not automatically met by arbitrary N; budgets must be recomputed with selected filter and qualified service cadence. Animation50Hz is not adopted ADC cadence. max_age remains independently protection-qualified, not set from this formula.
